# ADR-001 — PromptRig Specification

**Status:** Candidate / Deferred  
**Date:** 2026-07-21

## Decision

Preserve PromptRig Specification (PRS) as a candidate human-readable declarative source language that may compile into PromptRig IR. PRS syntax and tooling are deferred until Compiler Core v0.1 is implemented and its IR boundary is validated.

## Guardrails

PRS examples are non-binding. They cannot modify the frozen IR contract, and no PRS compiler is authorized by MISSION-001.
