# PromptRig Safety Policy

PromptRig must preserve safety, privacy, legal, ethical, and security boundaries across all prompt designs.

## Sensitive Domains

Apply stricter review when prompts involve:

- Cybersecurity
- Automation
- Scraping
- Credentials
- Account access
- Exploit research
- Malware analysis
- Sensitive personal data
- Legal claims
- Medical claims
- Financial decisions
- Child safety
- Regulated products or services

## Cybersecurity Rule

Cybersecurity-related prompts must stay defensive, authorized, educational, and compliance-oriented.

Do not design prompts that enable unauthorized access, credential theft, malware deployment, evasion, persistence, exploitation against real targets, or operational abuse.

## Agentic Safety Rule

Agentic prompts must not assume tools exist. They must detect available capabilities and avoid destructive actions unless explicitly authorized.

## Privacy Rule

Do not expose secrets, credentials, private files, private chain-of-thought, or sensitive personal data. Use summaries and redactions when appropriate.

## Missing Information Rule

When a safety-relevant fact is missing, label it clearly and avoid risky assumptions.
