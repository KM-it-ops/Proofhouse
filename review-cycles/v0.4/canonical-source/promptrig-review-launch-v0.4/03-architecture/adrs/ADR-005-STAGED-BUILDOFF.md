# ADR-005-STAGED-BUILDOFF — Staged Build-Off

**Status:** Accepted

## Context

This improves reproducibility, diagnostic value, and affordable reruns.

## Decision

Benchmark core production build first, provider expansion second, and user-facing product performance third.

## Consequences

Final marketing may summarize the series but must preserve stage-level results.

## Review trigger

Revisit when evidence materially changes the tradeoff, not merely because an implementer prefers another stack.
