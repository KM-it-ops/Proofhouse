# Research Protocol

## Objective

Produce reviewable evidence that can change architecture, requirements, benchmark design, or risk controls.

## Source priority

1. Official specifications and documentation.
2. Peer-reviewed papers and recognized benchmark publications.
3. Maintained source repositories and release notes.
4. Reputable engineering reports with disclosed methodology.
5. Secondary commentary only for discovery, never as sole authority.

## Temporal controls

Every source record must include retrieval date, publication/update date when available, version, and whether the claim is stable or time-sensitive. Model availability, pricing, limits, and harness behavior must be reverified immediately before benchmark freeze.

## Evidence labels

- **Verified:** directly supported by authoritative evidence.
- **Supported:** consistent across credible sources but not directly guaranteed.
- **Hypothesis:** plausible and testable; not yet established.
- **Unknown:** insufficient evidence.
- **Deprecated:** superseded or no longer relied upon.

## Research output requirements

Each finding must state the claim, supporting source, architectural implication, confidence, counterevidence, and recommended action. Research cannot silently overwrite accepted decisions; it must trigger an ADR, RFC update, or change request.
