# PromptRig Product Constitution

## Mission
Make high-quality AI system engineering accessible to people who cannot or do not want to become prompt engineers.

## Product Thesis
The durable value is not a better text prompt. It is a compiler that translates user intent into provider-aware, testable, portable AI-system artifacts.

## Design Principles

1. Plain language first.
2. Complexity should be earned.
3. Provider-neutral meaning, provider-specific implementation.
4. Evaluation before confidence.
5. Reversible actions by default.
6. Honest uncertainty and visible assumptions.
7. Open, portable core; paid convenience and operations.
8. One project model shared by Simple and Developer modes.
9. Reproducibility over marketing theater.
10. Generated systems must be usable, inspectable, and testable.

## Forbidden Product Behaviors

- Claiming a system is perfect or optimal without evidence
- Hiding unresolved evaluation failures
- Automatically performing consequential external actions without authorization
- Storing secrets in prompts or exports
- Misrepresenting which model or harness produced a result
- Ranking providers using incomparable conditions
- Locking user projects into non-exportable proprietary formats
