# Benchmark Marketing Claims Policy

Every public comparative claim must include or link to:

- benchmark and specification version
- exact execution dates
- harness and requested model
- observed model identifier or verification limitation
- number of runs
- autonomous or steered status
- environment and network mode
- score definition
- cost and duration basis
- known failures and interventions
- sealed commit and evidence package

Acceptable: “Across three autonomous Frozen-mode runs of PromptRig Benchmark v1.0, configuration A achieved the highest median nontechnical-UX score under the published rubric.”

Unacceptable: “Model A definitively builds better applications than every other model.”
