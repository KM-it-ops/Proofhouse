# Swap restaurant shifts without the group chat

Shiftcard is a phone app for servers, cooks, and bartenders who need someone to cover a shift in the next 48 hours.

## How a swap starts

You pick the date, station, and start time from the schedule already loaded for your location. Coworkers who share that roster see the open shift in a feed, newest first. Tap **Cover this shift** and Shiftcard checks whether you are already on the clock within 4 hours of that start. If you are clear, the request lands on the manager’s phone as one card: approve or decline.

## What changes for managers

One notification per request, not a 200-message thread. Approve moves the shift on both calendars and marks the floor plan slot filled. Decline asks for a short reason (for example “already down 1 cook that night”) that the poster reads in the app. In a 3-week desk test with a fictional 40-seat bistro, managers handled 14 swaps and tapped approve or decline in about 90 seconds each time. That test used fake staff names only; there are no public customers or revenue numbers here.

## What changes for staff

If two people request the same shift, the app shows queue position: first qualified request waits for the manager; the second sees **#2 in line** and can withdraw before anyone drives in. After approval, both phones get a calendar event and a one-screen summary you can screenshot for the tip-pool binder.
