# Swap restaurant shifts without the group chat

Shiftcard is a phone app where hourly staff trade shifts on a posted roster and the manager approves each swap in one tap—no side thread, no double booking.

## How a swap starts

Open the shift you cannot work (for example Tuesday 4 PM expo on the downtown location). Coworkers on that roster see it in a feed sorted by role match: only people tagged **grill** or **all stations** can tap **Cover this shift**. Shiftcard blocks the tap if you are already scheduled within 4 hours of that start. A valid request becomes a single card on the manager phone: **Approve** or **Decline**.

## What changes for managers

You see one push per request, not 47 messages in `#scheduling`. Approve updates both calendars and marks the station covered on the printed floor chart export. Decline requires picking a reason from 5 short options (such as “short staffed that night”); the poster sees it instantly. Shiftcard is invented for this example only—not a live app with real users or revenue. Scenario we wrote for demos: a 38-seat neighborhood spot runs 22 staff on 2 floors and logs about 9 swap asks per week. In that fiction, managers answered in 60–120 seconds because they never hunt for who offered first.

## What changes for staff

Two people can request the same shift. The first qualified coworker holds **#1 pending**; the second sees **#2 in line** and can withdraw before driving in. When the manager approves, both phones get a calendar invite and a summary screen with names, times, and station—handy for the tip-pool notebook. If the manager declines, the shift returns to the feed with the reason attached.
