# Tidewatch is ready for the dock

**Tide tables in your pocket, no signal required.**

We built Tidewatch for mornings when the marina Wi‑Fi is down and you still need to know whether the ramp is usable. Open the app, pick your station, and read high and low times without creating an account or watching an ad.

## What you get

- **Tidewatch** shows two years of predictions per regional pack. It **works fully offline with bundled tide data for two years** after you download the atlas at home.
- **No accounts and no ads.** Your favorites stay on the device; we do not run a login wall before launch day.
- **$4.99 one-time purchase** for the full station list in a region. No subscription, no auto-renew surprise.

## Who it is for

Trail runners crossing tidal flats, kayak guides timing a channel, and weekend skippers who would rather trust a chart than a flaky signal bar.

## Get it

Tidewatch ships this week on iOS and Android. Search **Tidewatch** in your store, grab the regional pack once on Wi‑Fi, and leave the hotspot in the glove box.
